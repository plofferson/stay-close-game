How to Play

You run at the same pace as Plofferson — tap Jump to clear obstacles!

  • Desktop: Space, Up arrow, or W to jump.
  • Mobile: Tap the JUMP button.
  • Jump over cars and the house. Don't fall in the trench!
  • Miss a jump and you'll hit an obstacle or fall off the screen — game over.
  • Level 1: Survive 60 seconds.

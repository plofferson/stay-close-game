How to Play

Level 1 — Stay Close

  • You run at the same pace as Plofferson. Jump to clear obstacles!
  • Desktop: Space, Up arrow, or W to jump.
  • Mobile: Tap the JUMP button.
  • Jump over jeeps, the tank, and the house!
  • Stay close to Plofferson to earn points. Survive 30 seconds to win.

Level 2 — Chopper

  • Tap (or Space / Up / W) to flap and gain height; gravity pulls you down.
  • Avoid buildings and mines. Don't hit the ground!
  • Your score is how many seconds you stayed alive. Survive 60 seconds to win.
